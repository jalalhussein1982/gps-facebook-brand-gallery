GPS FACEBOOK IMAGES - QUICK GUIDE

1. Choose one cover from covers/ and one profile image from profiles/.
2. Open the programme's Facebook page with your page-admin account:
   https://www.facebook.com/geopoliticalstudiesinPrague
3. Use Facebook's cover-photo or profile-picture edit control and upload the
   corresponding PNG. The exact control labels may vary.
4. Check Facebook's preview and reposition the image where needed. Make sure
   text remains visible and the circular profile crop looks right before saving.
5. After saving, check the page on both a phone and a desktop.

Covers: 2000 x 800 pixels, PNG.
Profile images: 1024 x 1024 pixels, PNG. Square files; Facebook applies its crop.
All eight files are standalone artwork, without gallery labels or buttons.
The gallery preview does not simulate Facebook's cover crop or interface.

Image credits are included in CREDITS.txt.
